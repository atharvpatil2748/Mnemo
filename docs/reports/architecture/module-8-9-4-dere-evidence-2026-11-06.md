## Protocol and conflict disposition

DERE-1 distinguishes (1) human-adjudicated semantic relevance, (2) explicit query-to-target engineering identity judgments, and (3) behavioral/security correctness. A match of an exact target identity can measure engineering retrieval of that declared target once a real query runs; it cannot prove semantic relevance. Positives must be authored before retrieval, not selected from its output. Negatives in this first case mean **outside the exact selected document/version tuple**, not semantically irrelevant. MRR/Recall@K/Precision@K are not computed without ranked output; nDCG requires defensible graded judgments and is not applicable here. Security, provenance, provider admission, deadline, and budget metrics are separate zero-tolerance checks. No model score is a relevance predicate.
