# Internal conduct standard

This standard applies to authorized Mnemo collaborators and project-operated
spaces. It is not an invitation for public contributions or a license grant.
The behavioral principles below were adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1;
that attribution is retained. See the [proprietary distribution policy](docs/governance/active/proprietary_distribution_policy.md).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Mnemo a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Welcome differing experience levels and technical perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, never people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and correct harm when mistakes occur.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
trolling, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject interactions
that violate this standard. Report conduct concerns privately through an
owner-authorized channel; do not publish private contact details. Maintainers will review reports promptly,
protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and apply proportionate action.

This standard applies in authorized project spaces and when representing the
project. Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
